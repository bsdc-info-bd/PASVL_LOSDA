# Trademark Policy

**Marks:** PASVL · LOSDA · Letter of Software Deployment Approval · Public Availability with
Source-Code Visibility License · Bangladesh Software Development Community · BSD C

**Owner:** Bangladesh Software Development Community (BSD C)
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Why this policy exists

The marks tell the world that a license, a permission, or a claim about compliance is genuine.
If anyone can use them freely, they stop meaning anything, and the people who rely on a
verification badge lose their protection.

This policy is designed to be permissive where honesty is served and restrictive where deception
is possible.

---

## 2. What you may do without asking

### 2.1 Nominal and nominative use

You may use the marks to refer accurately to the framework:

- "Licensed under PASVL-1.0."
- "Deployment requires a LOSDA."
- "Our LOSDA is LOSDA-BSDC-2026-000123."
- "This project follows the PASVL/LOSDA framework."
- "This work is stewarded by the Bangladesh Software Development Community."

### 2.2 License and permission statements

You may state, accurately, that a work is licensed under PASVL-1.0, and that you hold a LOSDA,
provided both statements are true at the time.

### 2.3 Badges

You may display the badges in [`assets/`](assets/) in accordance with
[`docs/badge-usage.md`](docs/badge-usage.md).

### 2.4 Education and commentary

You may use the marks in teaching, articles, criticism, and research about licensing.

### 2.5 Translations and derivative documents

You may translate and adapt the license and the framework documents under PASVL-1.0, provided
you:

- retain the attribution;
- do not present the adaptation as the official text;
- state clearly what you changed.

---

## 3. What you may not do

1. **Imply endorsement.** Do not suggest that BSD C endorses your product, your deployment, or
   your organization beyond the fact that you hold a permission.
2. **Imply stewardship.** Do not describe yourself as a steward, registrar, or issuer unless you
   are.
3. **Use the marks in a product name** in a way that suggests origin — for example, "PASVL Cloud"
   as a commercial brand.
4. **Register confusingly similar marks** — including domain names, social handles, package
   names, and company names.
5. **Alter the badges** in a way that changes their meaning (see `docs/badge-usage.md`).
6. **Use the marks to misrepresent a license.** Describing an MIT-licensed work as "PASVL" or
   vice versa.
7. **Use the marks in political advertising**, campaign material, or in a way that suggests the
   community takes a political position.
8. **Use the marks after a revocation** to imply that a permission is still active.

---

## 4. Logo use

The BSD C logo may be used:

- by BSD C;
- by works formally stewarded by BSD C;
- by event organisers, with written permission and a published code of conduct;
- in journalism and commentary about the framework.

It may not be modified, recoloured, or combined with another mark without permission.

---

## 5. Reporting misuse

Report misuse with [`templates/VIOLATION-REPORT.md`](templates/VIOLATION-REPORT.md), including a
screenshot and the URL. The Steward will:

1. contact the user and ask for correction;
2. allow 14 days for a response;
3. escalate to a platform complaint or a legal request if uncorrected.

---

## 6. Requests for permission

For anything outside this policy, write to the contact in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md),
stating:

1. the mark you want to use;
2. the context and the medium;
3. the duration;
4. whether it is commercial.

Permission, if granted, will be in writing and revocable on notice.

---

## 7. Relationship to the license

This trademark policy is **not** part of PASVL-1.0 and does not vary it. PASVL Article 17 states
the license's own position: no trademark rights are granted by the license, and nominative use is
permitted. This policy explains how the Steward applies Article 17.

---

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated by the Steward with a `CHANGELOG.md` entry. It will not be applied
retroactively to conduct that was permitted when it occurred.
