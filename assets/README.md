## 2. The mark

The mark means three things:

- **The brace is open** — the source is public and can be read by anyone.
- **The keyhole is shut** — deployment requires a key, and the key is a
  Letter of Software Deployment Approval.
- **The shield is closed** — the author's rights are protected, and the registry records who holds
  a key.

Colors: Bangladesh green (`#006a4e`) and red (`#f42a41`), with white linework. The mark works on
light and dark backgrounds; use the SVG version, not a raster export.

## 3. Rules

1. Do not alter the colors, proportions, or elements of the logo.
2. Do not place the logo inside another shape or combine it with another mark.
3. Do not use the logo to imply endorsement of a product.
4. Do not display a "LOSDA active" badge while the permission is suspended, expired, or revoked.
5. Full badge rules: [`../docs/badge-usage.md`](../docs/badge-usage.md).
6. Trademark policy: [`../TRADEMARK.md`](../TRADEMARK.md).

## 4. Using a badge

```markdown
[![PASVL-1.0](assets/badge-pasvl.svg)](LICENSE)
[![Deployment requires LOSDA](assets/badge-losda-required.svg)](https://github.com/bsdc-info-bd/PASVL_LOSDA)
```

For rendering on a hosted page, use the plain-text shields.io form described in
[`../docs/badge-usage.md`](../docs/badge-usage.md), or host the SVG yourself.

## 5. Creating derivatives

You may translate badge **text** while preserving the meaning and color convention. You may not
create a badge that implies a license status the work does not have.

## 6. Requests

For permission to use the logo outside the rules above, write to the contact in
