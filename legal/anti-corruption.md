# Integrity and Anti-Corruption Policy

**Version:** 1.0 · **Related:** PASVL §25.13, `governance/CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md`

A licensing framework is a small bureaucracy. Every bureaucracy is a target. This policy exists
to make the framework's decisions about permission, enforcement, and access resistant to
bribery, favouritism, coercion, and quiet influence.

## 1. Prohibited conduct

It is a Violation to offer, promise, give, request, or accept, directly or indirectly, anything
of value in order to influence:

1. the grant, refusal, scope, fee, or term of a LOSDA;
2. the timing of a LOSDA decision;
3. a decision to suspend, revoke, or reinstate;
4. an enforcement decision, appeal outcome, or Registry listing;
5. the appointment of a Registry Custodian, Council member, or appeal panellist;
6. the content of an amendment to the Licence.

"Anything of value" includes cash, gifts, hospitality beyond the threshold in Section 2,
discounts, shares, employment, contracts, referral fees, and favourable public statements.

## 2. Gifts and hospitality

| Value (fair market) | Rule |
|---|---|
| Under USD 50 | May be accepted and need not be declared |
| USD 50–200 | May be accepted; must be declared in the conflict register within 14 days |
| Over USD 200 | Must be declined, or accepted only as property of BSD C and disclosed publicly |
| Any value, if cash or cash equivalent | Must be declined |

Hospitality offered during a licensing negotiation that is not part of a public event must be
declined.

## 3. Conflict of interest

Every person who participates in a licensing or enforcement decision must:

1. declare any financial interest in the applicant, the Work, or a competitor;
2. declare any close personal relationship with a person involved;
3. recuse themselves from the decision;
4. record the declaration and recusal.

The register of declarations is maintained in `governance/` and summarised annually.

## 4. Facilitation payments

Facilitation payments — small payments to speed up a routine action — are prohibited absolutely,
even where local custom tolerates them and even where they may be lawful in a jurisdiction.

## 5. Government officials

Interactions with public officials in the licensing process must be documented. No gift or
hospitality may be offered to a government official beyond what is lawful and customary in the
official's jurisdiction, and never in connection with a licensing decision.

## 6. Procurement and Registry operation

Where the Steward contracts for Registry hosting, verification services, or legal services:

1. contracts are awarded on documented criteria;
2. a single vendor may not host both the Registry and the verification service without an
   independent audit right;
3. payments above the published threshold are disclosed in the annual report;
4. related-party contracts require License Council approval and public disclosure.

## 7. Whistleblowing

Any person may report suspected corruption confidentially to:

- the License Council's integrity contact (published in `GOVERNANCE.md`);
- if the Council is implicated, an external auditor named in the annual report.

Reports may be anonymous. Retaliation is a Violation and grounds for removal from any
framework role. A whistleblower's identity is protected to the extent the law permits.

## 8. Sanctions for corruption

1. Immediate suspension from any framework role;
2. Rescission of any LOSDA obtained through improper influence;
3. Permanent ineligibility for framework roles;
4. Referral to law enforcement;
5. Publication in the annual transparency report in aggregate, and in the Enforcement Register
   where a LOSDA was rescinded.

## 9. Record keeping

All declarations, recusals, gifts, and procurement decisions are retained for 7 years and are
available to an independent auditor.

## 10. Annual certification

Each Council member, Registry Custodian, and appeal panellist must sign an annual certification
that they have read this policy, declared all interests, and received no prohibited benefit.

## 11. Limits

This policy does not prohibit:

- ordinary professional networking;
- a paid LOSDA fee, which is a published commercial term;
- payment for genuine legal or technical services under a documented contract;
- an author's discretionary decision to issue a no-fee LOSDA, provided the decision is recorded
  and non-discriminatory.

## 12. Review

Reviewed annually. Amendments require License Council approval and a 30-day public comment
period.
