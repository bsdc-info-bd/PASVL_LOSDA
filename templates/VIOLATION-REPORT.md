# Violation Report

Use this form to report a suspected breach of PASVL-1.0 or of a LOSDA. Reports may be
confidential and may be submitted anonymously. Good-faith reports are protected; retaliation
against a reporter is itself a Violation.

**Send to:** the Author's contact in the Work's `NOTICE`, the Steward's integrity contact in
`GOVERNANCE.md`, or open a confidential issue where the repository supports it.
