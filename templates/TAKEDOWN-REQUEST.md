# Takedown / Platform Complaint Request

Use this form to prepare a platform, registry, or app-store complaint about unlicensed
distribution or deployment of a PASVL work. It is a **record-keeping template**, not a legal
document, and it does not replace a platform's own form.

> **Warning.** A copyright complaint has legal consequences for the person who files it,
> including liability for misrepresentation in some jurisdictions. File only where you own the
> rights, the material is genuinely infringing, and you have tried to resolve the matter
> directly first.

## 4. Why it is infringing

Common bases:

- [ ] The material is a verbatim or near-verbatim copy of the Work, distributed or deployed
      without a LOSDA.
- [ ] The material removes or replaces the copyright and licence notices.
- [ ] The material is presented under a different licence without authority.
- [ ] The material is a derivative work that reproduces protected expression.
- [ ] The material is a distribution of an executable form without a Class D LOSDA.
