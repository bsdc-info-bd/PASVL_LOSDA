# Governance of the PASVL/LOSDA Framework

**Steward:** Bangladesh Software Development Community (BSD C)
**Framework version:** 1.0
**Document version:** 1.0

A licence is only as trustworthy as the institution that maintains it. This document describes
that institution: who decides, how, and how to challenge a decision.

Detailed policies are in [`governance/`](governance/).

---

## 1. The bodies

| Body | Role | Composition | Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Steward (BSD C)** | Publishes the licence, operates the registry, holds the marks | Community organisation | [`governance/CHARTER.md`](governance/CHARTER.md) |
| **License Council** | Approves amendments, appoints officers, oversees integrity | 7 seats, staggered terms | [`governance/LICENSE-COUNCIL.md`](governance/LICENSE-COUNCIL.md) |
| **Registry Custodian** | Operates the LOSDA Registry and the Enforcement Register | 1 custodian + 2 deputies | [`governance/REGISTRY-CUSTODIAN.md`](governance/REGISTRY-CUSTODIAN.md) |
| **Appeals Panel** | Hears appeals against licensing and enforcement decisions | 3 panellists per case | [`governance/APPEALS.md`](governance/APPEALS.md) |
| **Ethics Review Board** | Advises on prohibited and high-risk uses | 5–7 members | [`governance/ETHICS-REVIEW-BOARD.md`](governance/ETHICS-REVIEW-BOARD.md) |
| **Authors' Assembly** | All adopters; elects and ratifies Council seats | Registered adopters | [`governance/ELECTIONS.md`](governance/ELECTIONS.md) |

## 2. Separation of powers

- **Authors** decide who may deploy their own work.
- **The Council** decides what the framework's rules are.
- **The Custodian** records what happened.
- **The Appeals Panel** checks that the others stayed inside the rules.

No body holds more than one of these powers in a contested matter.

## 3. What the Steward may and may not do

**May:** publish and version the licence; operate the registry; publish templates; appoint
officers; hold the marks; issue Continuity and Extension LOSDAs where the licence allows; publish
transparency reports; coordinate enforcement.

**May not:** grant permissions an author has reserved; revoke an author's LOSDA except as an
issuing authority; sell registry listings; take a share of authors' fees; hide decision-makers;
amend the licence without process; claim the licence is open source; discriminate.

Full list in [`governance/CHARTER.md`](governance/CHARTER.md) §§3–5.

## 4. Amendment

| Change | Process |
|---|---|
| Editorial | Steward + changelog |
| Clarifying | Steward + 14-day notice |
| Minor | RFC + 30-day comment + simple majority |
| Major | RFC + 45-day comment + two-thirds Council + Assembly ratification |
| Emergency | Immediate action + 7-day notice + ratification |

Rights already granted cannot be withdrawn. See
[`governance/AMENDMENT-PROCESS.md`](governance/AMENDMENT-PROCESS.md).

## 5. Appeals

Any affected person may appeal a refusal, scope decision, suspension, revocation, sanction,
registry correction, or anonymisation decision within 20 business days. Filing is free; the
target decision time is 45 business days. See [`governance/APPEALS.md`](governance/APPEALS.md).

## 6. Transparency

- Council minutes, redacted, published within 15 days.
- Every amendment logged in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
- Annual transparency report using
  [`governance/TRANSPARENCY-REPORT-TEMPLATE.md`](governance/TRANSPARENCY-REPORT-TEMPLATE.md).
- Registry and Enforcement Register publicly readable, free of charge.
- Independent audit of the registry at least every two years.

## 7. Finances

- Reading the registry is free.
- BSD C does not take a percentage of any author's licensing fees.
- Any administrative fee is published and must not be conditional on being listed.
- Annual accounts are summarised in the transparency report.

## 8. Conflicts of interest

Every officer declares interests annually and on arising, and recuses from conflicted matters.
See [`governance/CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md`](governance/CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md).

## 9. Integrity

Bribery, gifts over the threshold, facilitation payments, and favouritism in licensing or
enforcement are prohibited. Reports may be made confidentially and anonymously. See
[`legal/anti-corruption.md`](legal/anti-corruption.md).

## 10. Succession

If the Steward ceases to operate, a successor is named, or a trustee holds the registry in
escrow. Rights already granted survive. See
[`governance/DISSOLUTION-AND-SUCCESSION.md`](governance/DISSOLUTION-AND-SUCCESSION.md).
