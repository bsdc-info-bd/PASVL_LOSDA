# Elections and Appointments

**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Elected seats

Seats 1, 2, 3, 4, and 7 of the License Council are elected. Seats 5 and 6 are appointed and
ratified.

## 2. Eligibility to vote

| Seat | Voters |
|---|---|
| 1 — Individual authors | Natural-person authors registered as adopters in `registry/works.json` |
| 2 — Organisational authors | One representative per adopting organisation |
| 3 — Deployers | Licensees with an active LOSDA, one vote per licensee entity |
| 4 — Contributors | Contributors with a merged contribution in the previous 24 months |
| 7 — Bangladesh community | BSD C members in good standing |

One person may vote in multiple categories only where they hold genuinely distinct roles, and
never more than once in the same seat.

## 3. Eligibility to stand

A candidate must:

1. meet the eligibility criteria in `LICENSE-COUNCIL.md`, Section 4.1;
2. have been a registered adopter, licensee, or contributor for at least 90 days;
3. submit a statement of intent, a conflict declaration, and a commitment to the time
   requirement;
4. not be subject to an active Stage 2 or Stage 3 sanction.

## 4. Nominations

1. Nominations open 45 days before the vote.
2. Nominations close 21 days before the vote.
3. Each nomination requires one seconder from the relevant constituency.
4. The returning officer verifies eligibility within 5 days.

## 5. Campaigning

**Permitted:**

- a written statement published on the framework's site;
- answers to a standard questionnaire;
- public discussion of the framework's governance.

**Prohibited:**

- offering anything of value for a vote;
- promising a LOSDA, a refusal, or an enforcement outcome;
- misrepresenting the Licence or the framework;
- campaigning on behalf of a candidate without disclosure;
- using the BSDC marks to imply endorsement.

## 6. Voting

1. Voting is by secret electronic ballot, open for 14 days.
2. The method is single transferable vote where there are more than two candidates, and
   first-past-the-post otherwise.
3. Quorum is 20% of eligible voters. If quorum is not met, the election is rerun once; if it
   still fails, the seat is filled by Council appointment for one term.
4. The returning officer publishes turnout and results within 5 days.

## 7. Disputes

An election dispute must be filed within 10 days of the result. The Appeals Panel hears it and
may order a recount or a rerun. The result stands pending the decision, unless the Panel orders
otherwise in a case of apparent material irregularity.

## 8. Terms and rotation

1. Terms are two years, except seat 6 (three years).
2. Terms are staggered so that no more than four seats change in one year.
3. A member may serve a maximum of two consecutive terms, then must stand down for one term.
4. A by-election for a casual vacancy covers the remainder of the term.

## 9. Appointments (seats 5 and 6)

1. The Council publishes a role description and calls for expressions of interest.
2. A nomination committee of three (two Council members plus one external) shortlists.
3. The Council appoints by simple majority.
4. The Authors' Assembly ratifies within 30 days; a rejection requires a simple majority of
   those voting.

## 10. Removal

Removal follows `LICENSE-COUNCIL.md`, Section 10, and requires a hearing.

## 11. Recall

A petition by 25% of the relevant constituency triggers a recall ballot. A recall passes with
two-thirds of those voting, quorum 30%. A recalled member is ineligible to stand for one term.

## 12. Records

Ballots, nominations, and results are retained for 5 years. Personal data is minimised.
