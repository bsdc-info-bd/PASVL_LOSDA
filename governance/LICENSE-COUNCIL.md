# The License Council

**Version:** 1.0 · **Seats:** 7 · **Term:** 2–3 years, staggered

## 1. Role

The License Council is the framework's governing body. It does not license software — authors
do that. It governs the framework the authors rely on: the text of the License, the integrity of
the Registry, the fairness of enforcement, the marks, the money, and the succession.

## 2. Powers

1. Approve amendments under `AMENDMENT-PROCESS.md`.
2. Appoint and remove the Registry Custodian.
3. Constitute appeal and reinstatement panels and approve the roster.
4. Approve the annual transparency report and the audited Registry accounts.
5. Approve changes to governance documents, templates, and schemas.
6. Authorize contracts above the published threshold.
7. Decide framework-integrity matters referred by the Steward.
8. Interpret the License where a genuine ambiguity arises, publishing a reasoned opinion.
9. Approve emergency actions.
10. Approve dissolution and succession under `DISSOLUTION-AND-SUCCESSION.md`.

## 3. Limits

The Council must not:

1. issue or revoke a LOSDA for an Author's Work (except Continuity and Extension LOSDAs through
   the Steward's delegated authority);
2. overrule an Author's decision about their own Work, except on procedural grounds through the
   Appeals Panel;
3. take a share of an Author's fees;
4. change the License except by the Amendment Process;
5. act in a matter where a member has an undisclosed conflict.

## 4. Composition

| Seat | Represents | Elected or appointed | Term |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Individual authors | Authors' Assembly | 2 years |
| 2 | Organisational authors | Authors' Assembly | 2 years |
| 3 | Deployers and licensees | Licensee forum | 2 years |
| 4 | Contributors | Contributors | 2 years |
| 5 | Open-source and standards community | Appointed, ratified | 2 years |
| 6 | Legal and academic | Appointed, ratified | 3 years |
| 7 | Bangladesh community | BSD C members | 2 years |

### 4.1 Eligibility

A member must:

- have read the License and the LOSDA specification in full;
- have no unresolved conflict of interest;
- not be the subject of an active Stage 3 enforcement action;
- be able to attend at least 75% of meetings;
- disclose all framework-related interests.

### 4.2 Diversity

The Council must make reasonable efforts to achieve geographic, gender, and linguistic balance.
The nomination committee reports annually on the composition.

## 5. Officers

| Officer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chair | Chairs meetings, signs decisions, represents the framework |
| Vice-Chair | Deputizes and acts when the Chair has a conflict |
| Secretary | Keeps minutes, maintains the decision register |
| Treasurer | Publishes accounts, monitors the budget |
| Integrity Officer | Receives corruption and misconduct reports |

Officers are elected by the Council from among its members, except the Integrity Officer, who
may be an external appointment.

## 6. Meetings

1. At least quarterly, plus extraordinary meetings on 5 business days' notice.
2. Quorum: 4 members.
3. Ordinary resolutions: simple majority of those present and voting.
4. Amendment resolutions: two-thirds of the whole Council.
5. Removal of an officer: two-thirds of the whole Council, after a hearing.
6. Minutes published within 15 days, redacted for privacy and legal privilege.

## 7. Decision-making standards

The Council must:

1. state the question;
2. state the evidence considered;
3. record dissents;
4. publish reasons;
5. record the effective date;
6. identify the review date where relevant.

## 8. Conflict of interest

Members must declare and recuse under `CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md`. A member who fails to declare
may be removed under Section 6(5).

## 9. Reporting

The Council publishes:

- minutes;
- the decision register;
- the annual transparency report;
- audited accounts;
- an annual statement of the Council's own performance against its service targets.

## 10. Removal and resignation

A member may resign at any time in writing. A member may be removed for:

1. absence without explanation from three consecutive meetings;
2. failure to declare a conflict;
3. conduct that brings the framework into disrepute;
4. fraud, corruption, or a criminal conviction involving dishonesty;
5. becoming subject to a Stage 3 sanction.

Removal requires a hearing and two-thirds of the whole Council.

## 11. Vacancies

A vacancy is filled by the same route as the original appointment, within 60 days. Pending
filling, the Council may act with the remaining members if quorum is met.

## 12. Council decisions are public rules

A Council interpretation of the License is binding on the Steward and on Registry Custodians,
but is not binding on a court. It is published in `governance/interpretations/` with an RFC-style
record, and may be superseded.

## 13. Compensation

Council members serve voluntarily. Reasonable expenses are reimbursed against receipts and
published in aggregate. No member may receive compensation linked to licensing volume, refusal
rates, or fee revenue.
