# Appeals

**Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** LOSDA refusals, suspensions, revocations and scope decisions,
enforcement sanctions, Registry disputes, and data-protection decisions.

## 1. Right of appeal

Every person affected by a decision of an Issuer, the Steward, or a Registry Custodian has the
right to appeal that decision once. The right is exercisable whether or not the decision
mentions it. An appeal is not an act of hostility; it is the framework's quality control.

## 2. What can be appealed

| Decision | Appealable? | Appeal body |
|---|---|---|
| Refusal of a LOSDA | Yes | Appeals Panel |
| LOSDA scope or conditions imposed | Yes | Appeals Panel |
| Suspension | Yes | Appeals Panel (expedited) |
| Revocation | Yes | Appeals Panel |
| Exclusion from applying | Yes | Appeals Panel + Council ratification |
| Enforcement sanction (Stage 2+) | Yes | Appeals Panel |
| Registry record correction | Yes | Appeals Panel |
| Anonymised-listing refusal | Yes | Appeals Panel |
| Amendment decisions | Only on procedural grounds | Appeals Panel (procedural review) |
| Registry data-protection decision | Yes | Appeals Panel + supervisory-authority route |
| Stage 0/1 informal resolution | No — but the underlying NNC may be challenged | Appeals Panel |

## 3. Who may appeal

1. The party named in the decision.
2. A party materially affected by the decision, including a licensee whose sub-processor is
   affected or a Work's co-author.
3. A consumer body or non-profit acting on behalf of an affected person, with that person's
   consent.

## 4. Grounds

An appeal may rely on any of:

1. **Procedural unfairness** — no notice, no hearing, wrong reviewer, conflict of interest.
2. **Factual error** — the decision relies on facts that are wrong or incomplete.
3. **Wrong application of the instrument** — the Licence, LOSDA specification, or policy does
   not say what the decision assumes.
4. **Disproportionate sanction** — the sanction is excessive relative to the conduct.
5. **Discrimination or bad faith** — the decision was made on protected grounds or for an
   improper purpose.
6. **New evidence** — evidence that could not reasonably have been produced earlier.

## 5. How to appeal

1. Complete `templates/APPEAL-FORM.md`.
2. File within **20 business days** of receiving the reasoned decision, or within 10 business
   days for an expedited suspension.
3. Send to the Appeals Panel address in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
4. Filing is free.

Late appeals may be accepted where the delay is explained and no prejudice results.

## 6. Suspensive effect

| Decision appealed | Effect of appealing |
|---|---|
| Refusal | None — the refusal stands pending the appeal |
| Suspension | Suspension continues, but the Panel must decide within 10 business days |
| Revocation | Revocation is suspended during the appeal unless the harm is ongoing |
| Enforcement sanction | Sanction suspended unless urgent |
| Registry correction | The corrected record stands pending the appeal |
| Appeal against an appeal | Not permitted |

## 7. Panel composition and independence

1. **Three panellists**, at least one with legal training and one with domain expertise.
2. No panellist may be the original decision-maker, an employee of the same issuer, or an
   interested party.
3. Panellists declare conflicts under `CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md`.
4. Where the Steward is a party, the panel is drawn from a standing roster maintained
   independently of the Custodian.
5. Panellists serve without payment from the parties. Reasonable expenses are paid by the
   Steward.

## 8. Procedure

| Step | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acknowledge the appeal | 5 business days |
| 2 | Disclose the decision record to the appellant | 10 business days |
| 3 | Appellant's submissions | 15 business days from disclosure |
| 4 | Responding party's reply | 15 business days |
| 5 | Optional hearing (oral or written) | Within 20 business days of reply |
| 6 | Decision with reasons | 45 business days from filing |
| 7 | Publication | 10 business days after notification |

A hearing is held where the facts are disputed or the sanction is severe. Hearings may be
written, by videoconference, or in person in Dhaka, at the appellant's choice where practicable.

## 9. Standards of review

| Question | Standard |
|---|---|
| Facts | Whether the decision was reasonably open to the decision-maker on the evidence |
| Interpretation of the instrument | Correctness (the Panel decides what the text means) |
| Proportionality | Whether the sanction was manifestly disproportionate |
| Procedure | Whether the process was fair |

## 10. Powers of the Panel

The Panel may:

1. **Dismiss** the appeal.
2. **Allow** it and substitute a different decision.
3. **Remit** the matter to the original decision-maker with directions.
4. **Vary** the scope, term, or conditions of a LOSDA.
5. **Reduce or set aside** a sanction.
6. **Recommend** a change to a policy or to the Licence.
7. **Order** a Registry correction.
8. **Order** costs only in exceptional cases of bad faith, capped at documented expenses.

The Panel cannot create a permission the Author refuses to grant. It can require the Author to
decide again, fairly, and to state reasons.

## 11. Publication

Decisions are published with reasons, redacted for personal data and confidential information.
The Panel publishes an annual summary: number of appeals, categories, outcomes, and average
duration.

## 12. Second and final review

There is no second appeal. The Panel's decision is final within the framework. This does not
affect any right to a court, arbitration, or supervisory authority under the Licence (Article
24) or applicable law.

## 13. Reinstatement after a sanction

A party subject to a Stage 3 sanction may apply for reinstatement:

- **6 months** after the sanction, if the breach is cured;
- **12 months** after an exclusion, with evidence of a changed compliance posture;
- earlier if the sanction was reversed on appeal.

The Reinstatement Panel applies the same independence rules as the Appeals Panel.

## 14. Abuse of process

Repeated appeals on identical grounds, appeals filed to harass, or appeals containing knowingly
false evidence may be rejected as abusive. Rejection is itself appealable once.

## 15. Service targets and delay

If the Panel misses a deadline, the appellant may notify the License Council. If 30 further
business days pass without a decision, the appellant may treat the appeal as allowed for
procedural purposes, without prejudice to the substantive merits, and the Council must appoint a
replacement panel.

## 16. Records

Appeal files are retained for 7 years. The decision and reasons are retained permanently.
